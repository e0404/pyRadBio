# Brandt & Kitagawa (1982), Table II: mean ionization q(y_r) of ions

**Origin.** Table II (printed p. 5635) of the article cited below: the mean ionization
q = (Z1 - N)/Z1 against the reduced relative velocity y_r = v_r / (Z1^(2/3) v0), from a
Thomas-Fermi velocity-stripping criterion with b = 1.33 (24 rows, y_r = 0.024-5.932). Used
with the paper's eqs. (15) and (23) by `pyradbio/track/stopping/effective_charge.py`
(`effective_charge="brandt_kitagawa"` of `Bethe`).

**Citation.**

* W. Brandt and M. Kitagawa, "Effective stopping-power charges of swift ions in condensed
  matter", Phys. Rev. B 25, 5631 (1982), doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.25.5631.

**Retrieved.** From the maintainer's copy (Zotero item 2LG4MSVB).
`brandt1982_table2.csv` is a **hand transcription** of the printed table (printed strings,
read from a 400-dpi render); all 48 values were matched against the PDF's text layer.

**Notice.** The article is (c) American Physical Society. The transcription is raw source
data and is **not committed**; pyRadBio ships only its own interpolated table
(`pyradbio/track/stopping/data/brandt1982_ionization.csv`, written by
`scripts/stopping/build_tables.py`).
